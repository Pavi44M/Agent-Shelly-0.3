      The same core on every floor
      What every level shares, by design
      
  
        
    
      • Four lifts in the middle of the floor, so no desk or room is more than a short walk away.
        • 
    
      • Two fire stairs at opposite ends of the core, so there is always a second way out.
        • 
    
      • Washrooms on every floor: women's, men's and all-gender, an accessible washroom, and a parents' room on every working floor.
        • 
    
      • Risers and a cleaner's room beside the lifts: services and cleaning reach the floor without crossing the workspace.
        • 
    
      • Cleaning follows use: washroom visits are counted by door sensors (never cameras) and set how often each floor is cleaned.

How it is built

  

    
Business model. modules/gateway/gateway.yaml: sites, docks and racks, clients and contracts, fleet, the management team and every floor role. Change it and the page and 3D sites follow.

    
Numbers. shelly/gateway.py builds this week's service, stock, fleet, shipments, flags and decisions (synthetic, seeded by week) into docs/data/gateway.js. python -m shelly gateway prints the same summary.

    
Live site. docs/gateway/world.js (three.js): trucks book in, reverse onto docks and leave; forklifts unload to racks or pick for outbound; people work by role. Nothing here is real company data.

    
Brain. Gateway's agents sit in the Shelly Brain (warehouse, transport, cold chain, fleet, client accounts) and its approvals join the one approvals tray.

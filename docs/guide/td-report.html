Shelly guide

TD Report: daily Technology & Data newsletter

Every morning at about 6:15am NZ time, Shelly researches, verifies and emails a 5-minute briefing, then saves a copy to Google Drive.

At a glance

  • Sections: In 60 seconds · Retail & grocery NZ/AU · Consumer electronics · Wholesale, warehousing & production · AI, data & analytics · Markets (information only) · Roles that fit you
  • Every item needs 2+ independent sources (✓✓) or one official source (✓)
  • 'So what' lines give practical relevance for a store, an analyst or a future business owner
  • Learns from your 👍/👎 or 'more on X' replies

How it works

  1. Runs as a Claude scheduled task with standing pre-approval for a narrow set of actions: research, the Shelly Drive folder, reading your replies, and emailing only you.
  2. Keeps a Preferences & Feedback Log in Drive and applies changes once feedback is consistent.

Method & formulas

Verification
Items with one unofficial source are dropped; numbers are never estimated

How to read it

  • Reply to the email to tune tomorrow's edition.

Limits & safeguards

  • Markets content is information, not advice.
  • For fully unattended runs, turn on 'Automatically approve' in the task's settings.

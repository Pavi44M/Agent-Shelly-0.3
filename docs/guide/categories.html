Categories: P&L and budget

Category management is where commercial decisions are made: range, space, price and promotion.

At a glance

  • Sales, GM %, WoW, YoY, vs budget, waste and contribution per category
  • Diverging bar chart: behind budget (red) and ahead (blue)
  • Sortable table; tap a row to ask

How it works

  1. Contribution = gross margin − waste − markdown.
  2. Budget = the same weeks last year × (1 + growth %).

Method & formulas

Contribution
GM − waste value − markdown value
vs budget
sales − budget

How to read it

  • Look for categories that are behind budget and below last year. Those need a plan, not just a promo.
  • High GM % but low contribution means waste is eating the margin.

Limits & safeguards

  • Budget is derived from last year. A real business would load its own budget file.

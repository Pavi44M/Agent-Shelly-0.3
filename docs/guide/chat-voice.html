Shelly guide

Chat & voice

A conversational front door to the analytics, with voice in and out.

At a glance

  • Built-in engine: instant, offline, never invents numbers
  • Optional Claude API or Ollama for more natural answers (facts only)
  • 27 regions and languages for speech input and voice, e.g. NZ, AU, UK, US, India and South Africa English, Te reo Māori, Sinhala, Tamil, Hindi, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic and more
  • Speed and pitch controls; Test voice button; ▶ Briefing reads the week aloud

How it works

  1. Speech input uses the browser's speech recognition in your chosen language.
  2. Voices are grouped: best match for your region, same language, then other English voices. If a voice isn't installed on your device, Shelly says so and uses the closest one.
  3. With the built-in engine, written answers are in English. With Claude or Ollama, answers are written in your chosen language.

Method & formulas

Skill tag
Each answer shows which skill produced it, e.g. 'skill: retail.reorder'

How to read it

  • Tap 🔈 under any answer to hear it; 🔊/🔇 turns automatic voice on or off.

Limits & safeguards

  • Available voices depend on your phone or computer. Add more in its speech settings.
  • Voice input works in Chrome, Edge and Safari. Firefox doesn't support it yet.

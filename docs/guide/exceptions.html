Exceptions: what Shelly flagged

Exception reporting means you only look at what's unusual. Each flag has the evidence and a $ impact.

At a glance

  • Robust z-score against the same weekday over the previous 8 weeks
  • Sustained decline = last 14 days < 75% of the 8 weeks before. If it also fell last year, it's labelled seasonal
  • Shrinkage = physical count below SAP beyond tolerance; a count gain suggests a receiving or GR error

How it works

  1. For each product and day, Shelly compares the day with the median of the same weekday in the last 8 weeks.
  2. It uses the median and MAD (median absolute deviation) instead of mean and standard deviation, so one odd day can't distort the baseline.
  3. Flags worth less than $40 are dropped to reduce noise. The thresholds tune themselves from your decisions.

Method & formulas

Robust z
(x − median) ÷ (1.4826 × MAD)
Default thresholds
spike z ≥ 3 · stock-out z ≤ −3 · waste > 2× usual · decline < 75%

How to read it

  • A drop plus plenty of stock on hand points to a shelf or display problem, not a stock-out.
  • A shrinkage flag marked 'repeat' is worth a CCTV review; 'first time' may be a counting error.

Limits & safeguards

  • Daily data can't show time-of-day problems. Hourly POS data is on the roadmap.

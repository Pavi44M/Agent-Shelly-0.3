Shelly guide

Relevance check & connected agents

A good agent knows its limits. Shelly answers what it's built for and routes the rest.

At a glance

  • In scope: retail, electronics, wholesale, warehousing and production analytics
  • Out of scope: explains why and suggests the right agent type
  • Connected agents: Claude API, Ollama, or OpenJarvis / any OpenAI-compatible server on your computer
  • Hand-off needs your tap; only the question is sent, never store data

How it works

  1. The router first checks for clear out-of-scope intents (e.g. 'book me a flight'), then tries every skill.
  2. In Python: python -m shelly ask "…" prints the matching skill or the advice.

Method & formulas

Skill match
Share of the question's key words found in a skill's trigger phrases and description

How to read it

  • Replies from connected agents are labelled 'not verified by Shelly'.

Limits & safeguards

  • Keyword routing can miss unusual phrasing. Rephrase with a business term (sales, stock, order, margin…).

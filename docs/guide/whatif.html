What-if: price, waste and outage levers

A quick scenario model, the same maths as the Excel scenario sheet, for testing an idea before trying it.

At a glance

  • Price change −10% to +10%
  • Price elasticity −2.5 to −0.2 (default −1.2)
  • Waste reduction 0–60%
  • Outage days avoided per week

How it works

  1. Volume change = price change × elasticity.
  2. New sales = sales × (1 + price change) × (1 + volume change).
  3. New GM % assumes unit costs stay the same.
  4. Uplift = new GM − base GM + waste saving + margin on recovered delivery sales.

Method & formulas

Elasticity
% change in volume ÷ % change in price. −1.2 means a 1% price rise loses about 1.2% of volume

How to read it

  • If the uplift turns negative as elasticity moves towards −2, the price rise only works for loyal or convenience-driven lines.
  • Waste reduction is usually the lowest-risk lever.

Limits & safeguards

  • One elasticity for the whole store is a simplification. Categories differ a lot, so test on a few lines first.

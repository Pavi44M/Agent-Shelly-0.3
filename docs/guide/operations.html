Operations: reorder list and roster

The two daily jobs that most affect sales and cost: what to order and who to roster.

At a glance

  • Reorder point = demand over lead time + review period, plus safety stock
  • Order-up-to level is capped at average daily demand × shelf life
  • Status: 'Order now' (below lead-time cover), 'Order', 'OK' or 'Blocked, product recall'
  • Roster hours per day = forecast sales ÷ $ per labour hour, with a minimum per day

How it works

  1. Demand μ and variability σ come from the last 28 days.
  2. On-hand stock is estimated as the last physical count minus sales since that count.
  3. Suggested order = order-up-to − on hand, only when on hand is below the reorder point.

Method & formulas

Safety stock
z × σ × √(L + R), with z = 1.65 (≈95% service), L = lead time, R = review period
Reorder point
μ × (L + R) + safety stock
Roster hours
max(forecast sales ÷ sales-per-labour-hour, minimum hours)

How to read it

  • Search the table by product or supplier to build each supplier's order.
  • If the wage % is high on quiet days, the minimum-hours rule is doing the work. That's a staffing policy question, not a forecast problem.

Limits & safeguards

  • On-hand is an estimate between counts. Real SAP stock on hand would be more accurate.
  • Pack sizes and supplier minimums aren't applied yet.

Reports: Excel, PDF and presentation

Ask in plain words ("make a budget for the next 3 months", "stock report for dairy in Excel") or open one from the Reports section.

At a glance

  • Budget & forecast plan: 1, 3, 6 or 12 months; budget = last year + growth target, checked against Shelly's forecast
  • 13-week forecast: 4, 8 or 13 weeks with an 80% range from backtests
  • Weekly trading, category review, roster & labour, stock & reorder, waste & shrink
  • Industry packs: consumer electronics, wholesale, warehousing, production
  • Every report: whole store or any category; approvals section with sign-off lines

How it works

  1. Python computes every number (pandas, backtested forecasts); nothing is written by an AI.
  2. The report page renders the same spec three ways: Print (white paper), Dark (Shelly look) and Present (one section per screen, arrow keys).
  3. ⬇ PDF opens the print dialog: choose Save as PDF. On a computer, `python -m shelly report all --pdf` writes PDFs directly.
  4. Excel sheets: Dashboard (Category dropdown, KPI tiles, charts), Model (all formulas: SUMIFS, INDEX-MATCH, EDATE), Pivot (real PivotTables), Raw (Excel Table), Lookup, Assumptions (blue inputs), Notes (method, data dictionary, sign-off).
  5. Change the Category cell or any blue assumption and the whole workbook recalculates.

Method & formulas

Budget
Last year's same month × (1 + growth target)
Planning forecast
Last year's same period × recent 12-week trend (capped ±15%); weeks 1–3 blend in the daily models
Range
80th percentile of backtest errors (months: stand 3–6 months back; weeks: 13 weekly backtests)
Reorder
Safety stock = z × σ × √(lead + review); order up to capped by shelf life
Labour
Hours = forecast ÷ sales-per-hour target, never below the daily minimum

How to read it

  • Green/red KPI values show good/bad against target.
  • Low/High = where the forecast has landed 8 times out of 10.
  • Approvals list what needs a person's decision before it counts.

Limits & safeguards

  • Budgets need 12+ months of history for last-year comparisons.
  • Horizons snap to the nearest ready-made plan (e.g. 5 months → 6); the Excel lets you set any number of months.
  • Phone previews show numbers because formulas carry cached values; open in Excel to use PivotTables.

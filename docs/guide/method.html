Method & data quality

Every run follows the six CRISP-DM phases and leaves a validation report you can audit.

At a glance

  • 1 Business understanding: targets and thresholds in config.yaml
  • 2 Data understanding: quality score out of 100
  • 3 Preparation: column mapping for real exports
  • 4 Modelling: forecasts, exceptions, segments, reorder, roster
  • 5 Evaluation: backtests, stability checks, R cross-check
  • 6 Deployment: this site, Excel, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, WhatsApp, run report

How it works

  1. The quality score starts at 100 and loses points per issue (e.g. 0.5 per duplicate row, capped).
  2. The run report stores the SHA-256 of every input file, the config hash, and PASS/WARN/FAIL checks (freshness, beats the baseline, recalls blocked, P&L reconciles, cluster stability).

Method & formulas

Validation checks
Fresh ≤ 8 days · quality ≥ 80 · best WAPE ≤ seasonal naive · WAPE < 25% · recalls blocked · P&L reconciles · Jaccard ≥ 0.6

How to read it

  • WARN means look before trusting a specific output. FAIL means don't act on that part.

Limits & safeguards

  • The demo data is synthetic, generated to behave like a small Auckland convenience store.

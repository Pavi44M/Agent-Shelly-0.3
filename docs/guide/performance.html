Performance: daily sales, bridge and channels

Three views that explain why the headline number moved.

At a glance

  • Daily sales for 8 weeks plus the 7-day forecast (hover any day)
  • Sales bridge: last week → +volume → +mix → +price → this week
  • Delivery channels: Uber Eats and On-Demand per day for 14 days

How it works

  1. The forecast is the sum of the best model per category (see Forecasts).
  2. The bridge uses per-SKU prices and units in both weeks.
  3. Channel lines come from the channel column in the sales export.

Method & formulas

Volume
(units this week − units last week) × last week's average price
Mix
(Σ mix share this week × last week's SKU price − last week's average price) × units this week
Price
Σ (price this week − price last week) × units this week

How to read it

  • More units but a negative mix means customers bought cheaper lines. Check promos and range.
  • A channel dropping to zero for a day or more is almost always an outage (tablet, app, account), not lost demand.

Limits & safeguards

  • The bridge is a standard decomposition; small rounding differences are normal.
